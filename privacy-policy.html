Privacy Policy — Astervoid

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy describes how Astervoid ("the app"), operated by Neon Orbit Game Studio ("we"), handles information. Contact: neonorbitgamestudio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

The app requires no account and does not collect your name, email address, or contacts. Game progress, high scores, and unlocked content are stored locally on your device only, and are not transmitted to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device information including the advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This collection is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. See also how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices

Users in the EEA and UK are shown a consent form before personalized ads are served. On iOS, you can deny tracking through the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. Denying tracking does not stop ads from being shown, but limits their personalization.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, deleting the app removes all of it. We hold no server-side copy to delete. For data collected by Google AdMob, refer to Google's privacy policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to neonorbitgamestudio@gmail.com.
